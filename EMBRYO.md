# Embryo Sketch CAD

Embryo Sketch CAD is Embryo-Zero's fork of [SolveSpace](https://github.com/solvespace/solvespace) (e56db0d, commit e56db0d). It does parametric 2D/3D design.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING.txt) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
